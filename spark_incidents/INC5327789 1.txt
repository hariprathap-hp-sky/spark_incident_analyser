Impact Summary:
    - On 24th November 2025, between 05:55 and 12:29 UK time, customers in the UK experienced failures when activating eSIMs. Approximately 25% of activations failed. Physical SIMs were also potentially impacted, although volumes remained low.

Incident Management Summary:
    - On 24th November 2025, Incident Management was made aware that incident INC1234567 involved widespread eSIM activation failures caused by residual configuration data from numbers that were not fully de‑provisioned during a major site outage in the previous year. These numbers, which should have been cleared after quarantine, re‑entered the provisioning pool and triggered repeated failures.
    - The issue persisted as stuck MSISDNs were continuously selected during activation attempts, causing over 1,200 failed orders.
    - The support team performed manual LDAP cleanups in batches, identified and removed problematic numbers from OneNDS/SPS, and restored them to the available number pool. Replays were executed after cleanup, significantly reducing errors.
    - Root cause was traced to incomplete cease operations during the previous outage, and resolution required extensive manual intervention and coordination across teams.
    - Monitoring continues to ensure stability.
