u_impact_summary: On 24th November 2025, between 05:55 and 12:29 UK time, customers in the UK experienced failures when activating eSIMs on Sky Mobile. Approximately 25% of activations failed. Physical SIMs were also potentially impacted, although volumes remained low
u_incident_management_summary: On 24th November 2025,GS&R IM was made aware Incident INC5327789 involved widespread eSIM activation failures caused by residual configuration data from numbers that were not fully de-provisioned during a major site outage in November last year. These numbers, which should have been cleared after quarantine, re-entered the provisioning pool and triggered repeated failures. The issue persisted as stuck MSISDNs were continuously picked during activation attempts, leading to over 1,200 failed orders. The support team performed manual LDAP cleanups in batches, identified and removed problematic numbers from OneNDS/SPS, and restored them to the available pool. Replays were executed after cleanup, significantly reducing errors. Root cause was traced to incomplete cease operations during the prior outage, and resolution required extensive manual intervention and coordination across teams. Monitoring continues to ensure stability.
