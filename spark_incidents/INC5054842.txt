- Incident Management Summary:
'
    - On Tuesday, 12th August, GS&R Incident Management were engaged by NAP regarding widespread provisioning failures across four NAP sites (Basingstoke, Leicester, Manchester, and Gloucester). These issues were triggered by delayed responses from the Streamwide VSL platform following SSL certificate changes implemented on August 8th (CHG1437674).

    - The delays caused SSL handshake failures, remote host connection closures, and response timeouts. These errors led to transactions becoming stuck in memory, saturating heap space on the NAP side. The retry mechanism further compounded the issue by continuously queuing failed requests, overloading Kafka message queues and significantly increasing memory usage.

    - As a result of these failures, customers experienced degraded service performance, failed activations, and disruptions in number imports, with noticeable impact across UK and ROI regions.

    - Service was restored by NAP by restarting Streamwide pods across all four affected sites and checking memory utilisation on the nodes. This stabilised the connection to the VSL platform and cleared memory pressure.

    - Mitigation activities included restarting pods across all impacted sites, initiating TCP dumps for detailed traffic analysis, and monitoring temporary provisioning failures to identify recurring patterns or anomalies.

    - Monitoring was set up to track temporary failures and memory usage over time to detect early warning signs. An emergency change was raised retrospectively to formally document the live remediation steps taken during the incident.

    - The team agreed to continue monitoring the platform for 12 hours post-restoration to validate stability and avoid unnecessary memory scaling until the underlying root cause is fully identified and understood.
