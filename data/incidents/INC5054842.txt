u_impact_summary: Sky Mobile provisioning services may have intermittently failed for UK and ROI, including Activations, Cease and Number Importing ,and Landline voicemail provisioning are also impacted due to this incident 414 failed orders in UK OM. includes ACTIVATE/CEASE/RESTRICT 26 for ROI for activates
u_incident_management_summary: On Tuesday ,12th August , GS&R Incident management were engaged by NAP regarding a widespread provisioning failures across four NAP sites (Basingstoke, Leicester, Manchester, and Gloucester), triggered by delayed responses from the Streamwide VSL platform following SSL certificate changes implemented on August 8th (CHG1437674) . These delays led to SSL handshake failures, remote connection closures, and timeouts, causing transactions to get stuck in memory and saturating the heap on the NAP side. The retry mechanism compounded the problem by queuing failed requests, overloading Kafka message queues and further increasing memory usage. As a result, customers experienced degraded service performance, failed activations, and disrupted number imports, particularly in UK and ROI regions. Service was restored by NAP after restarting pods across all four sites and checked memory issues on nodes. Mitigation involved restarting pods across all sites, initiating TCP dumps for traffic analysis, and monitoring temporary failures to identify patterns.Monitoring was set up to track temporary failures and memory usage. An emergency change was raised retrospectively to document the live fixes, and the team agreed to continue monitoring for 12 hours to validate stability and avoid unnecessary memory scaling until the root cause is fully understood.
